The first full briefing is published on Monday 12 October 2026. This note explains what to expect.

## The question

Spending on AI data centres is growing faster than almost any infrastructure build in recent memory. The open question is whether demand will arrive to pay for it. This briefing does not start from an answer. Each week it gathers the evidence and says which way that week's news points.

## What each issue covers

- **Capacity**: new builds, expansions, land deals, and cancellations
- **Power and grid**: connection policy, power purchase agreements, and constraints
- **Cooling and design**: liquid cooling, rack density, and water use
- **Chips and supply**: GPU availability, pricing, and export rules
- **Networking**: optics, switching, interconnect, and subsea cables
- **Cloud providers**: capital spending, earnings, new regions, and pricing
- **Ireland and Europe**: planning decisions, grid and regulator policy, and EU rules
- **Deals and funding**: acquisitions, financing, and notable startups

## The verdict

Every issue ends with a one-line verdict, *bubble*, *buildout*, or *mixed*, with the reasoning behind it.

## The rules

1. Every number, quote, and date links to a source.
2. Unconfirmed reports are labelled as unconfirmed.
3. A quiet week gets a short briefing, not padding.
