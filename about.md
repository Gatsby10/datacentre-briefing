# About

**Bubble or Buildout** is a weekly briefing on the AI infrastructure boom: data centre capacity, power and grid, cooling, chips, networking, and the money behind them.

Hundreds of billions are being committed to AI data centres. Some of it will prove to be durable infrastructure, and some of it will not. Each week this briefing collects the evidence on both sides and gives a verdict.

## How it is made

An AI research agent monitors company announcements, regulator and grid operator notices, planning records, and earnings reports. It drafts the briefing, and a human editor checks it before it is published. Every figure links to the source it came from, and unconfirmed reports are marked as unconfirmed.

## Who writes it

Bubble or Buildout is written and edited by **Damola Abdullahi**, a cloud and infrastructure engineer based in Ireland, one of Europe's largest data centre hubs.

Nothing published here is investment advice.
