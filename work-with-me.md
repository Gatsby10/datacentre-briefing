# Work with me

I'm Damola Abdullahi, a senior cloud and DevSecOps engineer in Dublin. Alongside this briefing, I help two kinds of organisation.

## Small businesses in Ireland putting AI to work

Practical help for clinics, accountants, estate agents and other small firms that want AI to take admin off their staff:

- A staff workshop on using AI tools safely and well
- A properly set-up business AI workspace
- Automations for the jobs that eat time, such as missed calls, enquiries, reminders and document chasing

Eligible businesses can have part of the cost covered by the Local Enterprise Office [Grow Digital Voucher](https://www.localenterprise.ie/growdigital/).

## Startups that need production-grade cloud

AWS and Kubernetes infrastructure, CI/CD and security work, delivered as fixed-price projects:

- Moving to AWS or Kubernetes with Terraform
- CI/CD pipelines with security scanning built in
- Cloud cost reviews
